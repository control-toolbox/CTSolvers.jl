## Release proposal — CTSolvers v0.7.1 (stable)

**Version.** Last release `0.7.0`; last version in General `0.7.0`. Since `0.7.0`: new features and fixes, no breaking change. By `REL1` (0.Y.Z: the breaking component is Y) and `REL2` (no beta: the triple `0.7.1` is above every released one), the version is **`0.7.1`**.
**Jump in General (`REL3`).** None.
**Resolves from General alone (`REL4`).** ✅
**Versions applied.** ct-agents `v0.5.0` (this recipe), ct-conventions `v0.15.0`, CTActions `dc60661` (the release tool).

### What changes since `0.7.0`

- ✨ `reverse(r)` for an integration result: the same trajectory read in the other direction of time (#355, PR #356), needed by CTFlows to return every trajectory in increasing time.
- ✨ Option `barrier` of MadNLP.
- ✨ Accessors `nlp_model` and `ocp_model` of a built DOCP (#338).
- ✨ Readable display of `DiscretizedModel` and `BuiltModel` (#337).
- 🐛 `mu_init` and `mu_min` of MadNLP and MadNCL take effect (#321).
- Breaking: none. `BREAKING.md` holds a non-breaking note: `ocp_solution` is restricted to a built DOCP.
- Not in `[Unreleased]` yet, added by the release PR: the JET 0.13 `[compat]` widening (#315).
- Open PRs, not in this release: #342 to #353, the CompatHelper PRs.
